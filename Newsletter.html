01 / The big picture

What marked Expo 2026

Expo 2026 was more than an exhibition. It brought together businesses, entrepreneurs, innovators, investors, families, young people and members of the wider community.

Business meets community

Exhibitors showcased products and services, connected with potential customers and built relationships across Rwanda's private sector.

A platform for growth

Business exhibitions, electric vehicles, food, entertainment, innovation and children's activities created a space where business and community came together.

08 / Behind the scenes

The PSF staff understood the assignment

Behind every successful Expo is a team working before, during and after the event. PSF staff coordinated exhibitors, activities and stakeholders, helping create the experience visitors encountered on the ground.

09 / The people came through

A strong general turnout

Businesses, families, young people, entrepreneurs, consumers and visitors joined nearly 500 exhibitors from over 20 countries, turning the Expo grounds into a busy meeting point for business and community.

10 / Leadership perspective

A platform for business growth

“This is a premier platform for business growth, innovation, investment, and regional collaboration, let us forge new partnerships, and unlock opportunities that will drive sustainable economic growth and prosperity.”— Callixte Kanamugire, Acting Chief Executive Officer, PSF
11 / Appreciation

Credit to the security organs

Security organs helped maintain a safe and orderly environment for businesses, visitors and participants, supporting an atmosphere where people could attend, interact, shop, network and enjoy the Expo peacefully.

12 / Beauty & personal care

A different kind of Expo experience

The beauty and personal-care sector was visibly represented. Movit returned with “The World of Ubwiza”, while Johana Cosmetics showcased Made-in-Rwanda hair and skincare products. The visitor guide also listed exhibitors including Movit Africa, Johana Product, Manebu Industry, Dzetshal Rwanda, Ai Glamor Avenue and Roba Industries.
